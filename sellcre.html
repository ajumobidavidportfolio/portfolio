SellCRE turns one property address into a finished marketing package for commercial real estate brokers.

Before a property can be sold, a broker builds a package around it: an offering memorandum with property details, financials, maps and comparables, plus brochures, flyers, ads and emails.

I designed SellCRE from the ground up: the flows, the structure, every screen, the design system and the templates. I worked with the founder and the engineering team, built the front end myself, and as the product grew I grew the design team around it and led five designers.

Brokers now produce listing marketing over 90% faster, in one tool instead of five.

Workflow walkthroughs

The same numbers were typed five times.

Property details moved from reports into spreadsheets, documents and design files by hand. Every hand-off was a new chance for mistakes.

Broker conversations

Brokers wanted help writing, not generic AI copy.

They liked the idea of a first draft, but not words they couldn’t check or change before a client saw them.

What we didAI became an option, never a default, and every word it writes stays editable.

Every region has its own rules.

Marketing has to show details such as the brokerage name and licence number, and what’s required changes from region to region.

What we didA built-in database of regional rules, so each template adjusts itself and brokers don’t have to remember them.

Coordinator walkthroughs

Coordinators re-checked fonts, colours and logos on every file and rebuilt layouts whenever a broker asked for changes.

User flow

One mental model: upload, review, publish.

I mapped the whole journey before drawing a single screen. Every step falls into one of three stages, and the stage is always visible at the top of the app.

01 Start

  1. Sign up / log in
  2. Profile & brand kit
  3. Choose property type

02 Upload

  1. Enter property address
  2. Data fetched automatically
  3. Has reports?
    YesAI extracts from CoStar or PDFsNoSkip and continue
  4. Add property images

03 Review

  1. Check pre-filled details
  2. Use AI assist?
    YesAI drafts, broker editsNoWrite it manually
  3. Location & maps
  4. Underwriting & comparables

04 Publish

  1. Pick a template
  2. OM, brochure, flyer, ads and emails generated
  3. Refine in the visual editor
  4. Download, print or share

Key decisions

The calls that shaped the product.

Each one weighed speed against trust. Brokers put their name on every package, so control mattered as much as automation.

01

AI as an option, not autopilot

Considered: AI writes every summary automatically.

Brokers didn't trust copy they hadn't seen being written. Making AI a choice at each step, with every word editable, kept them in control and made them more willing to use it.

02

A guided wizard, not one long form

Considered: a single scrolling form for the whole OM.

An OM has dozens of fields. Five tabs with short sub-steps meant brokers only ever saw one small form, could stop and come back, and always knew what was left.

03

Financials in a grid, not form fields

Considered: separate inputs for every income and expense line.

Brokers think in spreadsheets. A familiar grid with automatic totals and five-year projections removed the step of rebuilding numbers in Excel.

Design system

One system behind every screen and 20+ templates.

Brokers handle multi-million-dollar properties, so the UI stays light and quiet and lets photos and numbers stand out. Under it sits a shared system of tokens, components and template rules that five designers worked from, mirrored one to one in code.

Components in the product

How the system works

01

Tokens first

Colour, type, spacing and radius live as variables in Figma and the same names in code, so a change in one place updates every screen.

02

Brand kits drive the templates

Each brokerage's logo, colours and fonts are captured once. Every one of the 20+ templates reads from the brand kit, so a new template is on-brand for every client by default.

03

One library for five designers

New patterns were proposed, reviewed and added to the shared library before use, so the product stayed consistent as the team and features grew.

Leading the work

From one designer to a team of five, without losing the thread.

I started as SellCRE's only designer. As the product grew, I hired and led a team of five and kept design and code moving together.

01

Clear ownership

Each designer owned an area: the wizard, the OM editor, templates or the marketing site. I owned the overall structure and the system that tied them together.

02

Critique and review

Regular design critiques and a review before anything shipped kept quality and consistency high across everyone's work.

03

Design to code

I turned the Figma designs into front-end code and handed it to engineers to connect to the back end. Features shipped faster, and what went live matched the design.

From one address to a finished OM, step by step.

Following one listing through the live product, from first login to a client-ready offering memorandum.

01 Onboarding

Set up once, on-brand forever.

A short two-step onboarding captures the broker’s profile, company logo and brand colours. Every template then uses them automatically.

02 Start a package

Pick a property type, type one address.

Templates and fields adapt to the asset type. Address autocomplete prevents typos, and the data starts loading straight away.

03 Auto-filled data

The rest fills itself in.

Property information, features and sale comparables are pulled in automatically. Brokers check and correct instead of typing.

04 AI data extraction

Drop in a report, AI does the reading.

Upload a CoStar report, rent roll or cash-flow sheet. AI extracts the details and fills the forms, and every value stays editable.

05 Review wizard

A guided wizard, never a giant form.

Brokers choose whether AI writes the summary or they write it themselves. Selling points are short, structured and always editable.

06 Underwriting

Numbers that feel like a spreadsheet.

Unit mix, income, expenses and five-year projections in a familiar grid, with totals calculated automatically.

07 OM editor

Client-ready, refined in a visual editor.

Every page of the offering memorandum in one list. Brokers reorder pages, swap templates and edit text and images right on the page.

08 Property dashboard

Every listing has a home.

Photo, wizard progress and every generated material for a property in one view, ready to open, download or share.

09 Marketing website

I also designed the site that sells it.

A homepage and feature pages for AI property data, AI financial data, OM creation and custom maps, written and laid out for brokers.

Beyond the happy path

Designed for when the data doesn't cooperate.

Real listings are messy. Every automated step has a clear way forward when it fails.

01

AI can't read a report

Fields it couldn't extract are flagged rather than guessed, and the broker can fill them in or upload a clearer file.

02

Address not found

Autocomplete prevents most typos. When public data is missing, the wizard switches to manual entry without losing progress.

03

Gaps before publishing

The property dashboard shows what's still empty in each tab, so nothing incomplete reaches a client.
