Fello rents thousands of iPads and POS kits to events through partners like Square, Shopify and Eventbrite. I designed the internal system that runs it all.

Fello IMS is where its sales, warehouse and returns teams work all day: orders, inventory, partner storefronts and team permissions, in one place.

The challenge

Thousands of devices, a dozen storefronts, one team.

Every rental touches sales, the warehouse and returns. Orders arrive from partner sites like Square and Shopify, devices need the right data plans and accessories, and every change has to be traceable.

Design goals

1

One order, one page.

Reservation, fulfillment, shipping and attachments in a single view.

2

Inventory at scale.

Track every iPad by barcode, serial and IMEI, and edit hundreds at once.

3

Control every storefront.

Products, pricing, services and sections per partner site.

4

Right access for each role.

Granular permissions for sales, warehouse, returns and admins.

5

Nothing changes silently.

A history log on every order, product, service and user.

How might we

give a fast-moving ops team one place to sell, ship and track thousands of rental devices without mistakes?

Components

Final product

One system, from order to check-in.

A walkthrough of the IMS, following a rental from the moment it’s ordered to the moment the devices come back.

01 Orders

Every order at a glance.

Orders from Fello, Square, Shopify, Eventbrite and more land in one list. Colour-coded statuses, rental dates and delivery methods make the day’s work obvious.

02 Order details

The whole rental on one page.

General info, reservation, fulfillment, shipping and attachments, each in its own card. View mode stays clean, and edit mode turns every field into a form.

03 Inventory

Track every device by barcode, serial and IMEI.

Items and models share one table pattern: searchable columns, filters and bulk actions, so huge lists stay quick to scan.

04 Bulk import

Hundreds of devices, added in one upload.

Upload a CSV with the rules spelled out up front, then review and fix flagged rows before anything is saved.

05 Bulk edit

Scan, then edit everything at once.

Scan barcodes or paste serials, IMEIs or phone numbers, then update shared fields for every scanned device.

06 Products & pricing

Every partner site, its own prices.

Products are set up per storefront with a days-by-quantity pricing grid, bundled pricing and visibility controls.

07 Services

Insurance, data and setup, configured once.

Services like LTE data, MDM, shipping and damage insurance can be per order or per product, with price variations and linked models.

08 Users & roles

Everyone sees exactly what they need.

Roles for admin, manager, sales, outgoing, returns, reports and payments, each with view, edit and action rights per page.

09 History everywhere

Nothing changes without a trace.

Orders, products, services, models and users all keep a searchable history: who changed what, when, and from what to what.
